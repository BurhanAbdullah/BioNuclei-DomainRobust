## Purpose

This analysis closes the previously open annotation-efficiency documentation gate using the already executed E6 evidence chain. It does **not** introduce a new training run. The four preregistered adaptation budgets are 1, 2, 3, and 8 labelled images from the authoritative 30-image Aitslab-bioimaging1 training split, corresponding to 1%, 5%, 10%, and 25%.

## Annotation-efficiency summary

For the prespecified primary metric AJI, the descriptive change from the 1-image budget to the 8-image budget is **-0.0128544 absolute (-18.83%)**. The observed E6 curve is therefore not monotonic in this executed protocol. The result should **not** be presented as evidence that additional annotation is intrinsically harmful: the experiment fixes a 20-epoch training budget, uses different adaptation subsets, and was designed to measure cross-dataset adaptation to Aitslab-bioimaging1 rather than to the locked S-BIAD634 test set.

The scientifically defensible reporting statement is that the executed E6 evidence does not demonstrate a monotonic benefit from increasing the labelled adaptation budget under the frozen protocol. This is an observed property of these four runs, not a general claim about annotation value.

## Leakage and scope controls

- S-BIAD634's locked 79-image zero-shot test set was not used for E6 adaptation.
- The Aitslab publisher test split remained isolated from adaptation.
- The development split was not used for training or selection.
- The four budgets were fixed before evaluation under the frozen E6 protocol.
- This analysis does not compare E6 values against S-BIAD634 E3/E4 values as if they were the same task/domain.

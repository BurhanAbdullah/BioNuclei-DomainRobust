Validation sequence

Evidence accumulated in staged gates.

Each stage has a defined role. Completed stages are described as completed; remaining publication gates are not silently promoted to finished results.

Verified

E1 · Source-domain baseline

Real BBBC039 baseline evaluation is retained, including the corrected RGB instance-mask decoder and held-out test evidence.

Verified

E2/E3 · Cross-domain diagnosis

S-BIAD634/S-BSST265 target data were acquired and profiled; zero-shot BBBC039-to-S-BIAD634 transfer and failure diagnosis are retained.

Verified

E4 · Domain robustness

Intensity-domain randomization was executed across the 79-image target pool with retained provenance and a matched image-level comparison against the diagnostic baseline.

Verified

E5 · Ablations and baselines

Controlled ablations, conventional baseline comparison, prespecified matched statistics and the aggregate integrity gate are retained.

Verified

E6 · Few-shot adaptation

The standardized 1%, 5%, 10% and 25% adaptation protocol was executed and archived. Annotation-efficiency analysis is retained separately and does not imply a monotonic benefit.

Verified

E7 · Independent validation

Independent BBBC038 validation was executed and retained. This supports external validation of the frozen workflow, not a claim of universal biological generalization.

Scope boundary

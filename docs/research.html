Research, experiments and evidence.

BioMCP is the broader research direction. BioNuclei provides the concrete scientific experiments used to study robustness, reproducibility and domain shift.

Research programme

What we are investigating.

01

Robust bioimage AI

Study nuclear instance segmentation across distinct fluorescence imaging domains.

02

Agentic analysis

Investigate reliable discovery and invocation of deterministic bioimaging tools.

03

Scientific interoperability

Develop explicit contracts between agents, tools, data and evidence.

04

Reproducible workflows

Turn validated operations into versioned and auditable studies.

05

Scientific memory

Preserve workflow context, parameters, results and failure modes across studies.

06

Intelligent experiment planning

Explore rule based and learned policies for choosing among valid workflow paths.

Evidence ledger

Results are promoted only when their evidence is traceable.

The public site deliberately separates verified evidence, descriptive diagnostics and research that is still in progress.

E1 · Source-domain baseline

The independently verified source-domain baseline reports Dice 0.9713 (95% CI 0.9695–0.9731), IoU 0.9443 and Boundary-F1 0.7098, with n = 50. Artifact: bbbc039-baseline-31995120233.

descriptive

E2 · Zero-shot cross-domain transfer

The committed failure-analysis record covers 79 target images and reports mean Dice 0.319 and median Dice 0.182, with p10 0.038 and p90 0.865. These values are descriptive diagnostic evidence, not a promoted final E2 result.

The experiment matrix distinguishes E1 source baseline, E2 zero-shot transfer, E3 domain-shift diagnosis and E4 the proposed domain-robust method. The homepage does not present these research-stage details.

E4 is the domain-robust method stage

The repository's experiment structure defines E4 as the domain-robust method, while the proposed method still needs to be defined, implemented, and validated from the observed failure modes. No uncommitted webpage-only metric is treated as an E4 scientific result.

Research integrity

Platform claims and scientific claims stay separate.

BioMCP is the platform vision. BioNuclei is a scientific workload and validation substrate. A benchmark result does not become a BioMCP platform result merely because an agent can eventually call the workflow.

What we are working on.

BioNuclei is a reproducible research programme for studying domain robustness in fluorescence bioimaging. The public record separates measured evidence from diagnostics, active experiments and proposed future work.

Current programme

From source baseline to independent validation.

The research sequence is deliberately staged so that each later claim depends on evidence established by earlier experiments.

Established workflow

E1. Source baseline

Train and evaluate the Boundary U Net baseline on the declared BBBC039 protocol with explicit validation and test separation.

Diagnostic

E2. Zero shot transfer

Evaluate the source model on held out target domain microscopy without target fine tuning to expose domain shift.

Diagnostic

E3. Failure diagnosis

Study intensity, geometry, scale, boundary and uncertainty related failure patterns before selecting an intervention.

In progress

E4. Domain robustness

Evaluate the robustness intervention under the fixed protocol. Execution alone is not treated as evidence of a scientific contribution.

Required

E5. Ablations and baselines

Remove individual components and compare against meaningful conventional approaches before interpreting any gain as a contribution.

Required

E6 and E7. Adaptation and external validation

Evaluate few shot adaptation and an independent external dataset only after the preceding protocol and method are frozen.

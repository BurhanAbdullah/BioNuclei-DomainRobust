An open research initiative

Reliable AI for biological imaging.

BioNuclei investigates how bioimaging AI can remain useful when image domains change, experiments are reproduced and scientific evidence is inspected. The current foundation is domain robust nuclear instance segmentation from BBBC039 to S-BIAD634 and S-BSST265.

The foundation

Start with a real scientific problem.

BioNuclei is first a reproducible research programme. It evaluates cross domain nuclear instance segmentation, documents dataset provenance and separates measured evidence from hypotheses. This scientific foundation comes before the larger agentic ecosystem.

Scientific substrate

BioNuclei gives the larger vision something real to test.

The current work focuses on cross domain nuclear instance segmentation in fluorescence bioimaging. The model, datasets, evaluation protocol and provenance are the measurable foundation on which later interoperability research can build.

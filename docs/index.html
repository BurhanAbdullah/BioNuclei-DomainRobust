An open research initiative

Reliable AI for biological imaging.

BioNuclei investigates how bioimaging AI can remain useful when image domains change, experiments are reproduced and scientific evidence is inspected. The current research foundation studies domain robust nuclear instance segmentation from BBBC039 to S-BIAD634 and S-BSST265.

Vision

Start with the scientific problem we want to solve.

We want bioimaging AI that is reliable beyond the conditions in which it was developed. That means studying domain shift, reproducibility, scientific provenance and failure modes while keeping the evidence inspectable. The broader vision is to build a foundation for trustworthy AI systems that can work with biological imaging tools and scientific workflows.

Current research

A concrete scientific test of the broader vision.

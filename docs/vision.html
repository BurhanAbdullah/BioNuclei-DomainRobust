VisionOpen research

Make scientific software work together.

BioNuclei begins with reliable bioimaging research and grows toward an interoperable scientific ecosystem. The goal is not another closed assistant. The goal is a way for researchers and AI systems to discover trusted software, execute explicit operations, inspect results and preserve the evidence.

Three principles

Reliability should be designed into the workflow.

01

Understand

Translate a scientific question into explicit analysis intent, data requirements and constraints.

02

Connect

Expose specialized bioimaging capabilities through typed and discoverable interfaces.

03

Prove

Record parameters, tool versions, inputs, outputs and validation evidence so a workflow can be inspected.

Scientific boundary

AI orchestrates. Scientific software measures.

The language model is positioned as a planning and interface component. Segmentation, quantification and scientific measurements remain grounded in explicit computational tools and validation procedures.

What the agent can do

Interpret a research question, identify suitable operations, coordinate a workflow and explain the resulting evidence.

What the agent must not replace

The scientific software that performs segmentation, measurement, visualization and other domain specific computation.

The sequence

Evidence comes before abstraction.

BioNuclei first establishes reproducible cross domain segmentation experiments. The wider BioMCP direction can then be evaluated against a real scientific substrate instead of a hypothetical demonstration.

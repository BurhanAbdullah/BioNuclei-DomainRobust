# S-BIAD634

## Role in the study

S-BIAD634 is the planned external fluorescence domain for zero-shot and few-shot evaluation. It is not used to tune the source-domain model before the zero-shot experiment.

## Authoritative source

BioImage Archive accession: **S-BIAD634**.

Official study page: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/bioimage-archive/galleries/S-BIAD634-ai.html

The BioImage Archive describes the study as an annotated fluorescence image dataset for nuclear segmentation. It contains raw fluorescence images and expert annotations and is released under CC0.

## Reported study characteristics

The authoritative BioImage Archive record currently describes:

- 388 files/images in the submission package.
- 79 expert-annotated fluorescence images.
- 7,813 nuclei in the 79 expert-annotated images.
- Normal and cancer samples from multiple tissue origins and preparation types.
- Heterogeneity in preparation, imaging modality, magnification, signal-to-noise ratio and related technical factors.
- Confocal fluorescence microscopy is listed as the imaging type for the study.

These values are **source metadata**, not experimental results generated by this repository.

## Evaluation policy

The source model trained on BBBC039 must be frozen for the zero-shot evaluation. No S-BIAD634 labels, metadata-derived class information, or target-domain images may influence model selection before the zero-shot score is recorded.

After the zero-shot experiment is locked, a separate few-shot protocol may use a predefined subset of S-BIAD634 for adaptation. The remaining target-domain groups must remain isolated for evaluation.

## Leakage controls

- Split by image/sample grouping rather than by randomly sampled patches.
- Do not allow crops from the same source image to cross train/test boundaries.
- Preserve the original target-domain grouping information where available.
- Record the exact file inventory and split manifest before training.
- Do not tune preprocessing thresholds on the final target test subset.

## Acquisition

Download through the BioImage Archive study page or its documented FTP/Aspera mechanisms. Raw third-party data must remain outside Git and must not be committed to this repository.

## Status

**Not yet acquired or validated in the execution environment.** No S-BIAD634 performance number is reported until the actual files and annotations have been inspected.

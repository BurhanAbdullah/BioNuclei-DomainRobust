BioFMResearch direction

Foundation models for bioimaging.

BioFM explores domain aware vision and multimodal models for microscopy and biological imagery, with robustness and scientific validity treated as first class requirements.

The research question

Can models understand the diversity of biological imaging domains?

Microscopy contains multichannel structure, staining variation, modality differences and strong shifts between acquisition environments. BioFM is the direction for studying models that can represent those differences while remaining useful for scientific tasks.

01

Domain awareness

Study representations that remain useful when imaging conditions, specimens or acquisition settings change.

02

Multimodal context

Explore models that can combine image content with metadata and scientific context where appropriate.

03

Scientific evaluation

Measure performance with explicit datasets, held out domains, task metrics and reproducible evidence.

04

Current substrate

Boundary U Net in BioNuclei provides the present concrete segmentation environment for domain robustness research. It is not an LLM.

Current status

Foundation models are a future research layer.

Research direction

A broad reusable bioimaging foundation model is not presented as an implemented contribution today. The project first establishes reliable experimental evidence and infrastructure.

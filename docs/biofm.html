Foundation models
for bioimaging.

BioFM is the research direction within the BioMCP ecosystem exploring domain-aware vision and multimodal foundation models for microscopy and biological imagery.

What is it?

Models that understand bioimaging domains

General-purpose vision models are rarely trained with microscopy's specific challenges in mind: multi-channel data, staining variation, imaging-modality shift and biologically meaningful structure. BioFM is the direction for models built with these constraints in mind, to eventually support tool selection and interpretation inside BioMCP workflows.

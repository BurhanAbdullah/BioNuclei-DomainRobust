Five distinct products and research directions.

The ecosystem shares a repository, but the products are not interchangeable. BioNuclei owns the scientific substrate and results. BioMCP is interoperability. BioFM, BioWF and BioSkills are separate research directions.

Rule: scientific benchmark numbers belong to BioNuclei. Agent interfaces belong to BioMCP. Future model, workflow and procedure directions stay in their own sections.

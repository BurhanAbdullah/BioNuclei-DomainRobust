BioMCP is the interoperability layer in a proposed ecosystem for agentic bioimaging. It connects planning with executable, inspectable scientific capabilities.

BioFM

Domain aware vision and multimodal model research. Models provide useful representations and reasoning signals without becoming the sole source of measurement.

BioMCP

Typed tool discovery and invocation. The bridge between an AI agent and concrete bioimaging capabilities, with inputs, outputs and provenance made explicit.

BioWF + BioSkills

Reusable workflows and domain instructions that make analysis procedures composable, inspectable and easier to reproduce.

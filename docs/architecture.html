ArchitectureResearch direction

A common language between agents and scientific software.

The proposed ecosystem connects planning with executable, inspectable scientific capabilities. Each layer has a defined role so that language model reasoning does not become a substitute for measurement.

Research ecosystem

Four layers, one scientific boundary.

01

BioFM

Future research on domain aware vision and multimodal models for biological imagery.

02

BioMCP

Proposed interoperability layer for typed discovery and invocation of bioimaging capabilities.

03

BioWF

Future workflow layer for versioned, reproducible and auditable scientific studies.

04

BioSkills

Future knowledge layer for reusable protocols, validation rules and failure analysis.

Tool surface

What a future bridge could expose.

02

Dataset quality

03

Preprocessing

04

05

From question to evidence

Every transition should remain inspectable.

01

Question

02

03

Discovery

04

05

Validation

Outputs are checked against the experimental protocol.

06

Evidence

Results and provenance are retained for inspection.

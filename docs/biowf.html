BioWF is the layer that turns individual BioMCP tool calls into complete, versioned, auditable scientific studies — with fixed protocols, seeds and provenance at every step.

What is it?

From a single tool call to a repeatable study

A scientific finding needs more than one correct tool invocation — it needs the same sequence to be repeatable, inspectable and comparable across runs. BioWF is the direction for composing BioMCP tool calls into workflows with explicit protocols, human checkpoints where needed, and complete provenance.

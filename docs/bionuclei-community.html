How it works

Four simple stages.

01 · UploadYour TIFF is accepted into a short-lived analysis workspace.
02 · AnalyzeThe configured BioNuclei Boundary U-Net pipeline runs on the server.
03 · InspectThe job status, instance count, result summary and provenance become available.
04 · DownloadDownload the complete ZIP or individual result files.

Research contribution
Help improve future BioNuclei models—only with your permission.
A research copy is never implied by using this service. When the checkbox is selected, the uploaded image is copied into a separate research-contribution namespace and linked to a SHA-256 fingerprint and consent metadata. The image can be deleted through the job endpoint.
Default
No research retention.
Opt-in
Explicit consent required.
